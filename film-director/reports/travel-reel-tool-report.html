Travel Reel Toolkit

Which open-source tools and connectors should power a "film director" agent that turns trip photos and short clips into a vertical reel cut to music? Below are the top picks per job, ranked by GitHub stars, 2026 activity, license and how easily they run headless on a CPU.

input photos + short clips output 1080×1920 · 9:16 length ≤ 00:01:00 cuts on the beat narration none
Verdictsummary

No existing project does this job end to end. The most-starred "AI short video" repos (MoneyPrinterTurbo, about 134K stars) build narrated videos from a script, using text-to-speech and subtitles. That is not what you want. So the plan is to reuse proven tools for each job and build the director layer that picks shots, matches them to the music and writes the edit plan.

ExifTool→PySceneDetect + OpenCV→Epidemic Sound→librosa / beat_this→edit plan (JSON)→FFmpeg xfade
01render

Editing engine and transitions

Pick: FFmpeg. It is the fastest renderer in a 2026 benchmark: 7.5 s for a 30 s 1080p template, against 86.6 s for MoviePy. Its xfade filter has 58 built-in transitions and zoompan gives slow zoom and pan on photos.

Transitions mapped to xfade: hard cut, fade/dissolve, fadewhite (flash), slideleft/smoothleft (whip-like), zoomin (punch-in), fadeblack. A true whip pan needs a short slide plus blur.

04story

Time, place and story order

Pick: ExifTool via PyExifTool reads capture time and GPS from photos and iPhone videos. reverse_geocode turns GPS into city names offline. Read metadata before any re-encode, because FFmpeg drops iPhone GPS when it converts MOV to MP4.

05music

Music and connectors

Pick: the Epidemic Sound connector, which is already connected in your Claude account. It searches more than 55K tracks by mood and BPM, and EditRecording cuts a track to an exact length (for example 60 s) with the drop pinned at a chosen time. It doesn't return beat times, so beat detection still runs locally.
  • The Creator plan covers Instagram only for non-commercial posts. Branded or sponsored reels need a business plan.
  • Free option: export a silent video and add a song from Instagram's own Sound Collection in the app.
  • Canva, through its connector, can make cover thumbnails. Google Drive can hold your raw media and the finished reels.
  • DaVinci Resolve MCP (about 3.8K stars) is the only mature editing MCP. It's an optional handoff to a professional editor.
06build

What we build ourselves

  • Ingest and score: raw media becomes ranked candidate shots, each tagged with time and place.
  • Planner: opens with the best shot as a hook, then follows the trip in order. Cuts land on beats, and shots get shorter when the music gets louder.
  • Edit plan (JSON): the contract between Claude and the renderer. Claude can review and edit it before rendering.
  • Renderer: FFmpeg normalizes every shot to 1080×1920 at 30 fps, then joins them with xfade transitions. Captions sit inside Instagram's safe zone.
  • Storyboard preview: a contact sheet you approve before the final render.
