process window@<b>process window</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">製程窗口</span><br>所有輸出都符合規格的參數範圍。<br><i>The process window shrinks when the uniformity spec is tightened.</i><br>均勻性規格收緊時，製程窗口會縮小。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">semiyield, sem-toolkit</span>
random vs systematic loss@<b>random vs systematic loss</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">隨機與系統性損失</span><br>隨機損失來自隨機落下的缺陷，跟 inline 缺陷密度走；系統性損失有固定圖樣或集中在某一層、某一區。<br><i>Systematic loss on one metal level kept growing while random loss stayed flat.</i><br>某一金屬層的系統性損失持續變大，隨機損失則維持不變。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield</span>
refractive index@<b>refractive index</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">折射率（n）</span><br>光在材料中速度的倒數比；SiO2 約 1.46，Si3N4 約 2.0。<br><i>A refractive index shift often signals a change in film composition.</i><br>折射率偏移常代表薄膜組成發生變化。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield, sem-toolkit</span>
response surface@<b>response surface</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">反應曲面</span><br>以二次模型描述輸出隨因子變化的曲面。<br><i>The response surface shows deposition rate rising with RF power.</i><br>反應曲面顯示沉積速率隨射頻功率上升。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">sem-toolkit, semiyield, metro-toolkit</span>
RF power@<b>RF power</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">射頻功率</span><br>電漿製程中供給的射頻功率，影響沉積與蝕刻速率。<br><i>Raising RF power increases PECVD deposition rate.</i><br>提高射頻功率會增加 PECVD 沉積速率。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">sem-toolkit, semiyield, waferlens</span>
short-term sigma@<b>short-term sigma</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">短期標準差</span><br>由移動全距或子群內變異估計，不含漂移。<br><i>EWMA limits use short-term sigma so drift cannot widen them.</i><br>EWMA 界限使用短期標準差，因此漂移不會把界限撐寬。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield</span>
silicon dioxide@<b>silicon dioxide</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">二氧化矽（SiO2）</span><br>最常見的介電質薄膜，可熱成長或沉積。<br><i>Thermal silicon dioxide is the classic metrology reference film.</i><br>熱成長二氧化矽是經典的量測參考膜。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield, sem-toolkit</span>
silicon nitride@<b>silicon nitride</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">氮化矽（Si3N4）</span><br>常作硬遮罩、間隙壁、保護層，n 約 2.0。<br><i>Silicon nitride on pad oxide is fitted as a two-layer model.</i><br>墊氧化層上的氮化矽用兩層模型擬合。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield, sem-toolkit</span>
specification limits@<b>specification limits</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">規格界限（USL／LSL）</span><br>客戶或設計要求的上下限。<br><i>The specification limits are 98.5 and 101.5 nm.</i><br>規格界限為 98.5 與 101.5 nm。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield, waferlens</span>
SPICE netlist@<b>SPICE netlist</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">SPICE 網表</span><br>描述電路元件與連接的文字檔，用於電路模擬。<br><i>Extracted device parameters are exported as a SPICE netlist.</i><br>萃取的元件參數匯出為 SPICE 網表。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">semiyield</span>
statistical process control@<b>statistical process control</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">統計製程管制（SPC）</span><br>以統計方法監控製程是否處於管制狀態。<br><i>Statistical process control flags the shift within four wafers.</i><br>統計製程管制在四片之內就偵測到偏移。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield, waferlens, sem-toolkit</span>
step coverage@<b>step coverage</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">階梯覆蓋率</span><br>溝槽側壁或底部膜厚與表面膜厚之比。<br><i>ALD keeps step coverage near 100 percent even at high aspect ratio.</i><br>即使在高深寬比下，ALD 的階梯覆蓋率仍接近 100%。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">semiyield</span>
synthetic data@<b>synthetic data</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">合成資料</span><br>以模擬產生、已知真值的資料，用於練習與驗證。<br><i>Synthetic data lets you score detection against a known truth.</i><br>合成資料讓你能以已知真值評估偵測效果。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield, waferlens</span>

thermal oxidation@<b>thermal oxidation</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">熱氧化</span><br>在高溫氧氣或水氣中成長 SiO2；常用 Deal-Grove 模型。<br><i>Thermal oxidation follows the Deal-Grove linear-parabolic model.</i><br>熱氧化遵循 Deal-Grove 線性-拋物線模型。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">semiyield</span>
thin film@<b>thin film</b>　<span style="color:#1c5cab">薄膜</span><br>厚度從次奈米到微米的沉積或成長層，如氧化層、氮化矽、high-k。<br><i>Each thin film in the stack is modelled with its own thickness and dispersion law.</i><br>堆疊中的每一層薄膜都用各自的厚度與色散模型來描述。<br><span style="color:#898781;font-size:smaller">metro-toolkit, semiyield, sem-toolkit</span>
