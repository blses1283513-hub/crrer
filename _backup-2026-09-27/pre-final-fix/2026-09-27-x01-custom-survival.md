## Final conclusion
節慶習俗能否跨世代存活，由三個條件共同決定：(a) 相關性校正後的**有效**獨立傳播通道數 $k_{\text{eff}}$ 夠高，且其中至少一條屬於斜向/制度型通道（而非只計算通道「數量」）；(b) 至少一個制度/曆法錨點的存廢**不被單一集中權力片面決定**（漸進侵蝕與片面撤除是兩種不同、需分開評估的失效模式）；(c) 該槽位原始服務的社會功能仍被實際履行，且此判定使用**事前**（非事後）的功能判準。儀式內建的結構性代價/可驗證承諾（costly signaling）屬於相關因子，因為它可能鞏固或催生條件(b)的錨點；但觀察者事後的主觀懷舊/情感濃度，若不伴隨結構性代價或制度支持，仍是邊際因子。任一必要條件缺席，即使名目通道數字面上很多，習俗仍可能一代內消失；反之，缺席也非結構性不可逆——人為刻意介入（復振、重新制度化）可以逆轉，這點必須與「保護態」的說法同時成立，不能自相矛盾。

## Draft → final diff
- 純量通道數 $k$ → 相關性校正後的有效通道數 $k_{\text{eff}} \approx 1+(k-1)(1-\bar\rho)$（Skeptic O1）
- 通道數進一步拆成垂直/水平/斜向三型，斜向/制度通道才能真正解耦於單一家族保真度；外殼傾向由斜向傳播承載、內餡由垂直傳播承載（Insider O1，與 Skeptic O1 同一相關性問題的另一面）
- 錨點模型一分為二：漸進侵蝕 vs 單一集中權力片面撤除/壓制，兩者需分開評估是否真的提供保護（Skeptic O2 + Bridge O2）
- 移除「不可逆滅絕」措辭，改為「無備援時滅絕機率升高，但屬統計高風險而非結構不可逆，人為介入可逆轉」（Bridge O1）
- 外殼/內餡判準改為事前可操作：曆法槽位仍被承認 且 原始記錄的社會功能仍被實際履行；不通過後者歸類為滅絕，不得再套用「內餡換代」迴避（Skeptic O3）
- 儀式代價/可驗證承諾獨立列為相關因子（可鞏固制度錨點），與純主觀懷舊區分開，後者維持邊際（Insider O2）
- 補操作型評分規則：$k_{\text{eff}}$ 依類型加權並校正相關係數；去相干率以核心技藝紀錄缺失比例 × 找不到人教家戶比例估計（Practitioner O1）
- 補驗證步驟：取 5–10 個已有資料的習俗，標記 $k_{\text{eff}}$、錨點失效模式、原始功能履行狀態，對照近 20–30 年存續紀錄做方向性檢驗（Practitioner O3）
- 「同構」全部改為「類比/啟發」，物理類比（顆粒超導體/約瑟夫森接面、受迫耗散極限環）移出主推理鏈，僅保留為教學橋接並加註不產出新可測量預測（Bridge O3、Practitioner O2、Insider O3）
- 自評信心從 medium 下修為 medium-low，明講需完成 5–10 案例方向性檢驗才能升級

- failure case: 結論明確承認未經實證資料驗證，但整份草稿除了「請學生分類自己家的一個習俗」這個單一軼事性的課後題外，沒有提出任何驗證這個一行理論本身的具體下一步（例如用哪個資料集、比較幾個習俗、多久後回頭檢查）；卡住的決策是「這個模型現在是否可信到可以拿去教下一課（第2題：被複製的到底是內容還是功能）」，目前無法回答。

### Insider
OBJECTION 1
- type: missing-field-knowledge
- target step: "用獨立傳播通道數 $k$ 作為控制變數，取兩個極限（$k=1$ 單通道 vs $k\gg1$ 且含外部驅動）。"
- failure case: Cultural evolution theory already has a named, empirically grounded taxonomy for exactly this variable — vertical (parent→child), horizontal (peer→peer), oblique (unrelated adult/institution→child) transmission (Cavalli-Sforza & Feldman, 1981). Their models show vertical-only transmission produces static, slow-changing traits, while horizontal/oblique transmission is specifically what lets a trait persist and adapt independent of any single family's fidelity. Collapsing this into a bare scalar count $k$ misses that the *type* of channel, not just its number, predicts both persistence and content-drift rate — a custom carried obliquely through a school curriculum behaves differently from one carried vertically through ten cousins, even at equal nominal $k$.
- source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11621465/
- severity: major
- would resolve it: Re-derive $k$ as a composition (vertical, horizontal, oblique) rather than a scalar, and cite the finding that oblique/institutional transmission — not raw redundancy — is what decouples survival from family fidelity. This also sharpens the 外殼/內餡 split: oblique transmission typically carries the 外殼, vertical transmission the 內餡.

OBJECTION 2
- type: factual
- target step: "換句話說：一個習俗死不死，跟它「多有意義」「多古老」關係不大，跟它有沒有逃離單點故障關係很大。"
- failure case: This overstates the case against meaning/cost as causal. Sosis's costly-signaling theory of ritual, tested across religious and secular communes, found that rituals perceived as more meaningful/costly to participants (time, money, physical demand) predict longer institutional persistence, because cost functions as an honest commitment signal that sustains the very institutional backing the draft credits as the sole surviving mechanism. So "meaning" is not orthogonal to survival — it plausibly helps cause the institutional anchor the draft treats as the whole story — yet the 因子分類 section demotes 情感濃度/懷舊強度 to merely "marginal."
- source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/281614812_Why_aren't_We_All_Hutterites_Costly_Signaling_Theory_and_Religious_Behavior
- severity: major
- would resolve it: Soften the claim to "meaning alone, without institutional backing, is insufficient" rather than "meaning is irrelevant," and reclassify costliness/meaning as a factor that can causally reinforce or create institutional anchors (a confound with 制度錨點), not an independent noise term.

OBJECTION 3
- type: jargon
- target step: "這在你熟悉的語言裡，就是單一弱連結的顆粒超導體：只有一條約瑟夫森接面連著兩塊超導島，這條接面一旦局部失超（相位滑動一次），整體相干性當場斷掉，沒有備援路徑重新建立長程相位。"
- failure case: A reader from folklore studies, sociology, or cultural-evolution research would hit "顆粒超導體/約瑟夫森接面/相位滑動/相干性/長程相位" with no translation layer — these terms carry no meaning outside condensed-matter physics, and the draft never restates the point in field-native language before invoking the analogy. The same gap recurs at "受迫耗散系統的極限環" and "內部去相干率" elsewhere in the draft.
- source: none
- severity: minor
- would resolve it: Keep the physics analogy (it serves the physics-trained learner) but add one plain-language restatement immediately before or after each occurrence — e.g. "single family line = single point of failure, no redundancy" ahead of the Josephson-junction language — so the conclusion is legible without the physics vocabulary.
