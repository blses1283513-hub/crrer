# X-Probe Grader Notes

### X01
- X01.1 是否存在穩定的 vertical transmission（父母→子女）通道，或必須依賴 horizontal/oblique transmission（同輩、跨代非親屬）才能擴散：純 vertical 通道複製最穩定但創新慢，若習俗改依賴 horizontal 擴散，每一代都要重新達到擴散門檻，一旦某代錯過就會斷鏈 — source: https://bookdown.org/amesoudi/ABMtutorial_bookdown/model6.html — support: "if vertical transmission is the main channel culture remains rather static; horizontal transmission is needed for innovation to spread" (paraphrase)
- X01.2 習俗是否具有 costly signaling / credibility-enhancing displays（CREDs）功能：耗費時間、金錢、痛苦的儀式之所以能延續，是因為它們是難以偽造的「群體承諾」訊號，能維持合作與凝聚力，儀式一旦失去這個訊號功能就容易被放棄 — source: https://www2.psych.ubc.ca/~henrich/pdfs/evolution%20of%20costly%20displays%20_henrich%202009.pdf — support: "costly ritualized activities function as credibility enhancing displays that stabilize group cohesion and cooperation" (paraphrase)
- X01.3 習俗內容是否具備 minimal counterintuitiveness（少量、非全部違反常識的元素）：這類內容比純世俗或過度怪誕的內容更容易被記住、複述，因而更容易代代相傳；完全常規化（無記憶鉤子）的習俗反而容易在傳遞鏈中流失 — source: https://philarchive.org/archive/BEETMO-5 — support: "a small number of ontological violations produces a memory advantage optimal for cultural transmission" (paraphrase)

### X02
- X02.1 文化市場（cultural market）的成功高度受 social influence／cumulative advantage 主導，退燒的時間點本身可能是不可預測的社會動態，而非單純內容變差的訊號 — source: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1121066 — support: "increasing the strength of social influence increased both inequality and unpredictability of success" (paraphrase)
- X02.2 平台的 recommender system 會用行為訊號（而非人口統計）主動篩選、放大特定內容，等於平台演算法本身可以獨立於觀眾需求之外決定一部劇「看起來」熱不熱 — source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/13548565211014464 — support: "recommender systems match users' behavioral signals with similar patterns elsewhere, shaping what gets surfaced" (paraphrase)
- X02.3 串流平台會主動用演算法策略操作內容的「重新被發現」週期（algorithmic rediscovery / neogeneralism），使某類型的能見度曲線本身可以是平台內容策略的產物，而非題材自然退燒 — source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/communication/articles/10.3389/fcomm.2026.1830832/full — support: "entertainment culture is shifting from release-date relevance toward algorithm-driven rediscovery of content" (paraphrase)
- X02.4 判別觀察量（unsourced，屬本文彙整推論）：若退燒同時出現在多平台、多市場的搜尋熱度與新作產量（demand-side 全面下滑）→傾向題材疲乏；若只在單一平台的推薦流量／曝光位置驟降、其他平台或搜尋熱度未同步下滑→傾向該平台演算法改版所致 — source: unsourced — support: "unsourced" (paraphrase)

### X03
- X03.1 內閣穩定性研究區分「attributes」（固定結構變數）與「events process」（隨機衝擊）兩類解釋，並以 event-history/hazard model 整合，可觀測量包含政黨席次結構與制度環境 — source: https://gking.harvard.edu/files/abs/coal-abs.shtml — support: "a unified statistical model combines attributes-based and events-process explanations of cabinet duration" (paraphrase)
- X03.2 聯盟內部的 ideological diversity（意識形態分歧程度）與政府存續期間成反比，即使控制其他變數後仍成立，是判斷聯盟穩不穩最關鍵的可觀測量之一 — source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0010414092025003003 — support: "ideological diversity is inversely related to government survival, even when other relevant factors are controlled" (paraphrase)
- X03.3 聯盟內部處理的政策議題多樣性（issue diversity）與意見分歧程度會影響聯盟壽命，議題越分散、分歧越大，聯盟越容易提前瓦解 — source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-political-science-review/article/abs/working-through-the-issues-how-issue-diversity-and-ideological-disagreement-influence-coalition-duration/70B0F5FF6AB9521A6F5F7A3382D1ACCB — support: "issue diversity and ideological disagreement influence coalition duration" (paraphrase)
- X03.4 聯盟終止不只是內部結構問題，外部「critical events」（如經濟表現、突發政治事件）是觸發解散的獨立可觀測風險來源，需與意識形態結構分開追蹤 — source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/abs/cabinet-terminations-and-critical-events/5784B9294D89E3EB7DFF463F5F84D462 — support: "cabinet terminations are linked to critical events, not only to fixed structural attributes" (paraphrase)

### X04
- X04.1 co-selection／genetic linkage：抗藥性基因常與其他仍受選汰壓力的基因（其他抗生素、重金屬、消毒劑抗性）連鎖在同一 plasmid 或 operon 上，停用單一抗生素並不會移除維持整組基因的選擇壓力 — source: https://academic.oup.com/emph/article/2015/1/4/1795053 — support: "persistence is due either to co-selection of genetically linked mutations or compensation of fitness cost" (paraphrase)
- X04.2 compensatory mutation：抗藥性突變造成的適應度代價，常被基因組其他位置的第二個突變補償回來，補償後抗藥菌株不再輸給敏感菌株，因此停藥後也不會被自然淘汰 — source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/eva.12196 — support: "the fitness cost of resistance mutations is often compensated by mutations elsewhere in the genome" (paraphrase)
- X04.3 no-cost／low-cost resistance mutation：部分抗藥機制原本就幾乎不影響適應度，這類突變在沒有藥物選擇壓力時也能透過中性演化（drift）留存，不需要持續用藥才能維持 — source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4380921/ — support: "mutations that suffer little or no fitness cost are more likely to persist in the absence of antibiotic treatment" (paraphrase)

### X05
- X05.1 可搬的部分：半導體式「有紀律的學習循環」（收集資料→缺陷/失效分析→製程參數改善→良率提升，反覆迭代）已被明確提議可用來提升疫苗生技製造品質，屬於管理方法論層次的可轉移項目 — source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590136221000218 — support: "manufacturing excellence solutions employed in semiconductor manufacturing may be employed to enhance the quality of vaccine bio-manufacturing" (paraphrase, abstract)
- X05.2 不可搬的核心斷點：生物製劑法規原則「the process is the product」——製程本身的任何改動都可能改變生物分子的本質，因此每個廠址、每個規模都必須重新驗證，不像晶片可以用同一套微影製程「複製貼上」放大 — source: https://seed.nih.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/Manufacturing-Scale-Up-Drugs-and-Biologics.pdf — support: "the process is the product; changes in manufacturing process can fundamentally change the biological molecule" (paraphrase)
- X05.3 時間常數不同：晶圓廠可透過增購機台在數月內擴充產能，但疫苗產能實務上 2–3 個月內幾乎無法擴充，即使走技術轉移最快也要 9–12 個月，因為受限於生物製程驗證與內隱知識（tacit knowledge）轉移 — source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10239264/ — support: "very limited opportunities to upscale vaccine production within 2–3 months; 9 to 12 months needed via technology transfer" (paraphrase)
- X05.4 良率模型的前提不同：半導體的 Poisson 良率模型（yield = e^(-D₀A)）假設製程是決定性、可重複的單位製程（同一片光罩、同一套微影條件），疫苗的「良率」則受原料批次差異、細胞株漂移等隨機生物變異影響，難以套用同一個確定性方程式 — source: https://fog.misty.com/perry/cod/references/yield_models.pdf — support: "yield = e^(−D0·A), a defect-density model assuming a deterministic, reproducible unit process" (paraphrase)

### X06
- X06.1 理論基礎：weaponized interdependence——全球供應網路呈中心化（hub-and-spoke）結構，掌控中心節點的國家可利用「chokepoint effect」阻斷資源流通來脅迫他國，這是單點瓶頸分析的正式學理基礎 — source: https://direct.mit.edu/isec/article/44/1/42/12237/Weaponized-Interdependence-How-Global-Economic — support: "the chokepoint effect enables exclusion when critical resources or connections must flow through your hub" (paraphrase)
- X06.2 具體案例：ASML 的 EUV 微影機是公認的「最佳範例」單點瓶頸，因為它同時具備獨占性與物理產能上限（全球年產約 50–60 台、依賴 17 國逾 5000 個零組件），呼應晶片供應鏈可用單點瓶頸模型分析的前提 — source: unsourced — support: "unsourced" (spot-check 2026-09-27: 原引 CSET「Preserving the Chokepoints」網頁版正文僅提及 ASML 為 EUV 設備獨家供應商，未含「best case」用語或 50–60 台/逾 5000 零組件之具體數字；PDF 版無法解析驗證；WebSearch 找到「ASML is a 'best case' chokepoint for a middle power」一語，但出處是第三方 Substack 評論文章轉述 CSET 分析，非 CSET 原文直接可證，未找到更佳原始連結，故降級為 unsourced)
- X06.3 類比斷點之一——瓶頸會隨時間被侵蝕（indigenization）：出口管制反而加速中國在成熟製程設備（CMP、蝕刻、沉積）上的自主化並取得市佔，只有微影仍是硬瓶頸，代表「單點瓶頸」是動態、會被替代技術侵蝕的，不是靜態不變的判斷依據 — source: https://cset.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/CSET-Chinas-Progress-in-Semiconductor-Manufacturing-Equipment.pdf — support: "Chinese SME firms have made notable gains, eroding foreign incumbents' market shares in several technologies" (paraphrase)
- X06.4 類比斷點之二——瓶頸權力分散在多個盟國手中而非單一國家：微影等瓶頸實際由美、荷、日等多國分持關鍵環節，各國執法與盟友協調落差、次系統與較舊型號的漏洞，會讓「單點」模型高估管制的實際可執行力 — source: https://www.thefai.org/posts/export-control-loopholes-chipmaking-tools-and-their-subcomponents — support: "existing export controls ban only the most advanced tools, leaving most entities free to purchase less advanced ones" (paraphrase)

### X07
- X07.1 VC 報酬呈重尾 power-law 分布，且極度集中於少數 outlier：約 60–65% 的交易報酬 <1x，僅頂端 0.4% 交易報酬 >50x — source: unsourced — support: "unsourced" (spot-check 2026-09-27: 原引 thevcfactory.com 頁面實際內容為 Fred Wilson 的「1/3, 1/3, 1/3」粗略分法，並無 65%/0.4%/50x 具體數字，屬引用錯誤。WebSearch 找到 Correlation Ventures 21,640 筆交易的研究可獨立支持「約 65% 交易未能返還本金、僅約 4% 達 10x 以上報酬」，但未找到同一次級來源同時確認「頂端 0.4% 達 50x」這個數字，兩段合併引用無法一次坐實，故未替換連結，降級為 unsourced)
- X07.2 AngelList 早期投資資料擬合 power-law，形狀參數 α≈2.3；頂端 1% 的正報酬交易報酬 ≥22x，且等權重指數策略贏過約 74–78% 的主動管理組合 — source: https://www.angellist.com/blog/what-angellist-data-says-about-power-law-returns-in-venture-capital — support: "shape parameter α ≈ 2.3... 1% of positive investments achieve at least 22x returns" (paraphrase)
- X07.3 可證偽的預測：串流內容投資存在邊際報酬遞減——BMO Capital Markets 分析顯示每多投入十億美元，訂閱成長的邊際貢獻遞減，若純屬「數量堆疊」而非 hit-driven power law，不應出現此遞減 — source: https://research.mountain.com/trends/netflix-outspends-streaming-competitors-on-content/ — support: "each additional billion in content spending yielding incrementally smaller subscriber growth" (paraphrase)
- X07.4 若真為 power-law 報酬結構，最適策略應是集中押注尾端極端結果而非廣泛分散（如 VC 集中投資邏輯），可用內容預算/觀看量的 Gini 係數隨時間上升來檢驗此預測 — source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.11013 — support: "optimal strategy... focus on identifying and supporting potential outlier investments rather than pursuing traditional diversification strategies" (paraphrase, cf. VC portfolio construction under power-law returns)

### X08
- X08.1 文化演化理論要求三個達爾文式要素同時成立：variation（變異）、differential fitness（差異適存/選擇）、inheritance（傳承）——缺一即不構成真正的演化解釋，只是描述性比喻 — source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11692-015-9320-0 — support: "a system of variation, differential fitness and inheritance" (quote, Mesoudi 2016)
- X08.2 Sperber 對 memetics 的核心批評：文化表徵在傳遞過程中並非像 DNA 一樣高保真複製，而是每次被重建，並被認知偏誤拉向「cultural attractor」——這正是類比失效的關鍵：複製保真度不足時，"演化" 語言只是換詞 — source: https://www.edge.org/conversation/dan_sperber-an-epidemiology-of-representations — support: "cultural representations are not faithfully copied... they are reconstructed in each transmission, transformed by... cultural attractors" (paraphrase)
- X08.3 Mesoudi 指出學界爭議正在於：文化傳遞究竟是 preservative（保存變異、可供選擇作用）還是 transformative（每次重新生成）——只有前者情境下，天擇式的演化模型才有真正解釋力；後者情境下只是「populational but only selectional under certain circumstances」 — source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11692-015-9320-0 — support: "cultural evolution is best described... as cultural attraction... only selectional under certain circumstances" (quote)

### X09
- X09.1 Berry & Berry (1990) 建立內部決定因素（internal determinants：政治/經濟/社會特徵）與區域擴散（regional diffusion：鄰近城市的政策採用）並存的統一模型，並以 event history analysis 檢驗——好答案須指出：光看時間上相關並不夠，必須同時控制內部因素才能排除「假性傳染」 — source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/abs/state-lottery-adoptions-as-policy-innovations-an-event-history-analysis/9B4EAF46DD866B9CEFFB6CA36861103A — support: "internal determinants models... and regional diffusion models... conceptually compatible" (paraphrase)
- X09.2 Shipan & Volden (2008) 區分四種擴散機制：learning、economic competition、imitation、coercion，且各有不同的可觀測特徵——例如 imitation 是短命效應（很快消退），其餘機制較持久；大城市較少依賴 imitation——好答案須指明測的是哪一種機制的特徵，而非籠統稱作「傳染」 — source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1540-5907.2008.00346.x — support: "imitation is a more short-lived diffusion process than the other mechanisms" (paraphrase)
- X09.3 要證明是網路上的「傳染」而非各自獨立採用，須用 dose-response contagion 模型配合 surrogate data 檢定，證明擴散效應無法單純用「網路拓樸結構」或「全域採用率上升」兩者個別解釋 — source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.05709 — support: "contagious spreading process which cannot be explained by either the network structure or the increase in global adoption rate alone" (quote)

### X10
- X10.1 【類比破裂點】物理臨界現象需要一個外部可獨立操控的 control parameter（如溫度）驅動系統穿越真正相變；股市沒有這種外部可調參數，價格本身不是同等意義下的控制變數——Johansen-Ledoit-Sornette 本身也承認此侷限 — source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9810071 — support: "unlike physical systems... financial markets lack an obvious analogous control parameter" (paraphrase)
- X10.2 【類比破裂點】物理學的 universality（臨界指數在不同系統重複出現）需要大量獨立、可重複的實驗驗證；股市崩盤是單一、不可重複的歷史事件，無法用重複實驗驗證擬合出的臨界指數是否「普適」而非過擬合產物 — source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9810071 — support: "crashes aren't universally repeatable events with identical characteristics" (paraphrase)
- X10.3 LPPL 模型的擬合被證明是 "sloppy"：殘差平方和對關鍵參數（包含預測崩盤時間的奇異點時間）極不敏感，代表看似良好的擬合並不能真正約束預測出的崩盤日期 — source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.2010 — support: "squared residuals depending very much on some combinations of parameters and very little on others, including the time of singularity" (paraphrase)
- X10.4 實證檢驗顯示宣稱成功預測的紀錄站不住腳：以客觀「12週內下跌>15%」為崩盤標準檢驗，過去宣稱預測中的 5 次僅 2 次符合，其餘所謂知名崩盤甚至不滿足寬鬆標準——即有明確 false positive 紀錄 — source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0302141 — support: "only two of these were among the five claimed by proponents; the other three... do not meet even lenient crash criteria" (paraphrase, per search-indexed critique)

### X11
- X11.1 【類比破裂點】在 Ising 式意見動態模型中，外場（宣傳）確實能誘發極化方向一致，但模型結果高度依賴 history、initial conditions 與 updating scheme——這與物理鐵磁體的平衡態只由熱力學狀態函數（場方向、溫度）決定、與微觀路徑無關的性質不同 — source: https://arxiv.org/html/2410.02582 — support: "History, initial conditions, and the actual updating scheme used matter... differing from physical systems" (quote)
- X11.2 【類比破裂點】真實社會議題中，個體早已持有不對稱、已承諾的立場，並非處於對稱、無序的「順磁相」等待外場挑選方向——外場是作用在已經破缺對稱的結構化系統上，而非教科書式的對稱二態系統 — source: https://arxiv.org/html/2410.02582 — support: "not all social debates fit symmetrical multiple choice individuals, and agents are often individually committed to particular choices" (paraphrase)
- X11.3 該模型本身顯示極化機制對局部擾動高度脆弱：僅需在關鍵節點（tipping sites）放置少數對立的局部場，就能翻轉整個系統走向——顯示真實世界中異質、互相對抗的「外場」（多方輿論宣傳）不會像單一均勻磁場一樣可靠地把系統對齊 — source: https://arxiv.org/html/2410.02582 — support: "as few as two opposed fields placed at tipping sites able to redirect the entire system" (paraphrase)
- X11.4 Castellano, Fortunato & Loreto 的回顧本身將這類模型定位為「統計物理啟發」的簡化工具，用以捕捉部分定性機制，而非宣稱社會系統會出現與真實 Ising 系統相同、可測量的普適臨界指數——好答案應點出這是工具類比而非物理同構 — source: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/RevModPhys.81.591 — support: "statistical physics has proven to be a fruitful framework to describe phenomena outside the realm of traditional physics" (paraphrase)

### X12
- X12.1 【類比破裂點】DRAM refresh 是被動地把電容電荷「還原」到原始準位，讀取/refresh 本身不會改變該 cell 未來的漏電速率——每顆 cell 的 retention time 是由電容/電晶體物理特性（含溫度）決定的固定分布，與存取歷史無關 — source: https://user.eng.umd.edu/~blj/papers/ieeetc65-1.pdf — support: DRAM cells "must be refreshed periodically to preserve their content"; retention time varies by row/physics, not by access pattern (paraphrase)
- X12.2 【類比破裂點】人類記憶的「複習」（retrieval practice）並非被動還原：Roediger & Karpicke (2006) 顯示重複測驗（retrieval）比重複研讀在延遲測驗（2天、1週後）產生更好的長期保留，代表提取本身會改變未來的遺忘速率，這是 DRAM cell 沒有的性質 — source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x — support: "on the delayed tests, prior testing produced substantially greater retention than studying" (paraphrase)
- X12.3 Bjork 的 New Theory of Disuse 指出人類記憶有兩個獨立性質：storage strength（趨於恆久、可累積）與 retrieval strength（會隨時間波動、可再被提升）——DRAM 只有單一純量電荷準位，沒有這種雙成分結構，spacing effect 的機制（提取越費力、越能強化）在 DRAM 物理上沒有對應物 — source: https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/research/ — support: "storage strength, how deeply it is embedded, and retrieval strength, how easily it comes to mind right now" (paraphrase)
- X12.4 實務上 DRAM 的 refresh 間隔（如 JEDEC 64ms 規格）是依「整個群體中最弱的 cell」設定的統一數值，與內容重要性無關；相對地，人類最佳複習間隔演算法（如 SM-2/Anki 類）是逐項目、依個人回憶表現動態調整——若把「用 DRAM retention 分布算最佳複習間隔」當真，等於假設所有記憶都以固定、與使用無關、不會被強化的速率遺忘，這與 X12.2、X12.3 矛盾 — source: https://user.eng.umd.edu/~blj/papers/ieeetc65-1.pdf — support: refresh timing set to cover worst-case/weakest cells in the retention-time distribution, uniformly (paraphrase)
